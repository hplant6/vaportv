# vaporTV guides

How to get things working in vaporTV. The same guides are built into vaporTV, under
**Settings → Help → Guides**, each with a QR code that opens it here on your phone.

1. [Streaming apps](streaming-apps.md): Netflix, Disney+, YouTube, Plex, Kodi and the rest
2. [The Video tab](video.md): Your Emby or Jellyfin films and shows, and adding your own
3. [The Music tab](music.md): Your music from this TV, drives, shares, your phone or your server; playlists, radio and background music
4. [Finding and organising your games](games.md): The top bar and More, each tab's list, sorting, favourites, playlists and search
5. [Emulators](emulators.md): EmuDeck, where games go, and each console's tab
6. [GOG, Epic and other PC games](pc-games.md): Heroic Games Launcher, and games added to Steam
7. [The controller](controller.md): Every button, the on-screen keyboard, and moving buttons
8. [The Netflix and Twitch extension](netflix-extension.md): Moving around Netflix and Twitch with a remote or D-pad
9. [TV remotes](remotes.md): Pairing, the buttons, and waking the box
10. [Claude on vaporTV](claude.md): Talking to Claude on the TV, signing in, and the voice search and music it powers
11. [Making it yours](look-and-feel.md): Backdrops, your own videos, the screensaver, Home's corners and background sounds
12. [The Files app](files.md): Browse the box, USB drives and SD cards; open pictures, videos and songs; copy and move files
13. [When an update breaks something](updates.md): Repairs, and updating vaporTV
14. [Privacy](privacy.md): What vaporTV sends where, what it keeps, and clearing it
15. [Support vaporTV](support.md): Tipping on Ko-fi, and what's coming next
