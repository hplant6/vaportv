# Finding and organising your games

Find any game in vaporTV, from the top bar to search, and keep the ones you play close with favourites and playlists.

## The top bar

The bar along the top holds **Home** and up to nine more tabs. Press **L1 / R1** to move between them. A tab only shows once it has something in it: a console's tab once it has games and its emulator, **Video** once you've signed in to a media server.

When there are more tabs than fit, the rest go on the **More** screen, the last tab in the bar. It shows them as tiles, as many as you have. Rest on a console's tile and the console starts to turn. Press **A** to open one; **B** from a tab you opened there takes you back to More.

To arrange them, open **Settings → Tabs**. It has three lists: **Top bar**, **More** and **Off**.

1. Pick a tab and press **A** to pick it up.
2. Move it **up** or **down**, within its list or over into the next one.
3. Press **A** to put it down. **B** puts it back where it was.

Home is always first. The top bar holds nine tabs besides Home and More; moving one up into a full bar sends the bar's last tab to More. Tabs under **Off** don't show anywhere.

## A tab's list

Each console's tab, **Steam** and **Arcade** has a list down the left, under the console's logo (the console itself turns slowly behind it). The grid beside it shows whichever list you're on.

| List | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **All games** | Every game on the tab |
| **Recents** | What you've played, most recent first |
| **Favorites** | The games you've starred with **Y** |
| **Playlists** | The playlists set to show on this tab |

Each list shows how many games it holds, such as **Favorites (12)**.

- **Right** or **A** on a list steps into its grid. **B** goes back to the list.
- **Sort by** at the top orders the lists by **Name**, **Last played**, **Most played** or **Date added**. Recents always stays in the order you played them. Each tab remembers its own sort.
- **L2** hides the list, so the grid has the whole width. Press it again to bring the list back. Each tab remembers this too, and which list you were on.

**Settings → Consoles** sets how each tab lays out its games.

## Arcade and Neo Geo

The **Arcade** tab's list has a **Systems** section after its playlists, one list per board: **Atomiswave**, **NAOMI**, **NAOMI 2**, **CPS1**, **CPS2**, **CPS3** and **MAME**. The logo at the top follows the system you're on.

To take a system out of the list, switch it off in **Settings → Consoles → Arcade tab systems**. Its games stay in **All games**.

**Neo Geo** games have a tab of their own, rather than being on the Arcade tab.

See [Emulators](emulators.md) for where arcade games go and how to name them.

## The Steam tab

The **Steam** tab has the same list: **All games**, **Recents**, **Favorites**, **Sort by** and your playlists. Under **Playlists** it also has:

- **Your Steam collections**, the ones you choose in **Settings → Steam → Steam tab collections**.
- **Non-Steam games**: games added to Steam from elsewhere, such as Heroic or Lutris. See [PC games](pc-games.md).

Favorites on the Steam tab are vaporTV's own, separate from Steam's.

## Favourites

Press **Y** on a game in its tab to make it a favourite, and again to take it off. It goes in that tab's **Favorites** list.

## Playlists

Playlists are your own lists of games. There are two kinds:

- A **Home playlist** is a row on Home. It can mix games from any system, and films and episodes from the Video tab too (see [The Video tab](video.md)).
- A **tab playlist**, such as a **SNES playlist**, is a list on that tab, for that console's games only.

**To add a game to one:**

1. Press **X** on the game (or **Start**, then **Playlists**).
2. Its playlists are listed: **Home playlists**, then that tab's. Pick one and press **A**: **Add** becomes **Added**. Press **A** again to take the game out.
3. To start a new one with the game in it, choose **New Home playlist** or **New SNES playlist** (named after the game's console) below the list, and type its name.

**To manage them**, open **Settings → Home → Game playlists** and pick a playlist:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Rename it |
| **Move up / Move down** | Change its place in the order (and its row's place on Home) |
| **Show on** | Where it shows: **Home**, and any tab. Switch each on or off |
| **Games** | Every game in it. Switch one off to take it out |
| **Delete playlist** | Press **A** twice. The games stay |

**New playlist** at the bottom of the list starts an empty one.

> **Tip:** a playlist can show in more than one place. Switch on **Home** and a console under **Show on**, and it's a row on Home and a list on that tab.

## Home

**Recents**, the first row on Home, holds what you've played lately, games and apps together, so you can carry on with one press. Your Home playlists follow lower down, in the order set in Settings.

## Search and voice search

To find any game or app by name:

- On a controller, press the **right stick** (R3). You can change this button in **Settings → Controller**.
- On a TV remote, tap **Menu**. (Hold Menu for Settings.)

Search opens listening. Say a game or app, such as "Metroid Prime". Say "play Halo 2" and it starts straight away, when the match is clear. Choose **Type** to type instead, or the microphone to speak again.

What you say is turned into words on the box itself; the recording isn't sent anywhere. The speech model downloads the first time you use it.

If Claude Code is installed on the box, the words it heard also go to Claude, which helps work out what you meant: a half-heard title, a game you describe ("the blue hedgehog one"), or a kind of game ("something co-op"). See [Claude](claude.md).

> **Tip:** on the Music tab, the same button searches your music instead.

## Game options

Press **Start** on a console game. It shows its playtime, when you last played it and its size, then:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Playlists** | Add it to a playlist or take it out (see **Playlists** above) |
| **Change name** | Rename it, if the file name reads badly |
| **Change emulator** | Open it in a different emulator or RetroArch core, where there's more than one |
| **Change artwork** | Pick its cover from SteamGridDB, RetroArch's box art, or a web search |
| **Delete files** | Remove it from the drive. Press **A** twice; it can't be undone |

Press **Start** on a **Steam** game for its playtime, when you last played it, its achievements and its size, then:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Playlists** | Add it to a playlist or take it out |
| **Open in Steam** | Its page in your Steam library |
| **Verify files** | Steam checks the game's files and repairs any that are broken. Not for non-Steam games |

## If something's wrong

- **A console's tab is missing:** it has no games yet, or its emulator isn't installed (see [Emulators](emulators.md)). Or it's on the **More** screen, or under **Off** in **Settings → Tabs**.
- **A list is missing from the Arcade tab:** check **Settings → Consoles → Arcade tab systems**.
- **A playlist doesn't show where you expect:** check **Show on** for it in **Settings → Home → Game playlists**.
- **A game isn't offered a playlist:** a tab playlist only takes that console's games. Use a Home playlist to mix systems.
