# The controller

Every button on the controller and what it does in vaporTV, and how to move them to other buttons.

## The buttons

This guide uses Xbox names. vaporTV shows your own controller's buttons on screen: on a PlayStation controller **Cross** is A, **Circle** is B, **Square** is X and **Triangle** is Y. On a Nintendo controller the buttons go by where they sit, so the bottom one (Nintendo's B) chooses.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **D-pad / left stick** | Move |
| **A** | Open what's highlighted |
| **B** | Back, or close what's open |
| **X** | Playlists: put the game, film or show you're on in a playlist, or take it out |
| **Y** | Favourite: on a game, film or show, add it to Favorites or take it off; on an app, put it on Home or take it off |
| **Start** | Options for what you're on: **Game options** for a game, what an app opens in for an app, details for a film or show, a song's menu for a song |
| **Select** | Settings (press it again to go back Home) |
| **L1 / R1** | Previous or next tab |
| **L2** | Show or hide a tab's list on the left |
| **R2** | Show what each button does, along the bottom of the screen (press again to hide) |
| **L3** (click the left stick) | Next song |
| **R3** (click the right stick) | Voice search |
| **Both sticks clicked in** | Sound off, or back on |
| **Hold Select + Start** | Close the game or app in front and come back to vaporTV |
| **Hold R3 + RT** (right stick in, right trigger) | Report a problem, from wherever you are |
| **Steam button** (the Xbox or PS button) | Steam's menu |

A few notes on these:

- **L3** skips the song you're hearing: the Music tab's when it's playing an album or playlist, otherwise the background music. The song's name comes up at the bottom.
- A button that's part of a two-button press (**L3**, **R3**, **Select**, **Start** and **RT** at first) acts as you let go, not as you press. That's so pressing a pair doesn't also do what each button does alone: clicking both sticks turns the sound off without also skipping a song or starting voice search.
- **Both sticks** turn off the background music and ambience, and anything playing in Files. Do it again to bring the music back.
- **Hold Select + Start** for about a second. It works whatever is in front: a game, a streaming app or an emulator.
- **R2** shows your buttons as they're set now, so a button you've moved shows in its new place.
- **Hold R3 + RT** for about a second to open **Report a problem** straight over the screen you're on, so its details show where the problem is. It works while vaporTV is on screen: from a game or app, close it first.

## In a game or app

Once a game or app is open, the controller belongs to it. vaporTV doesn't act on any button until you're back, except two:

- **Hold Select + Start** closes it.
- The **Steam** button opens Steam's menu, as always.

A streaming website needs a controller layout in Steam before the controller works in it. See [Streaming apps](streaming-apps.md).

## Different places

The buttons mostly do the same everywhere. Where they don't:

### Home and the tabs

- **L1 / R1** go along the tabs in the top bar. Past the last one they stop on the power button, then go round to Settings.
- **L2** hides a tab's list on the left for a bigger grid. Press it again to bring it back.
- **Start** on a game opens **Game options**: rename it, pick its emulator, change its art or delete it. On a Steam game it shows its playtime, achievements and **Open in Steam**.
- **Start** on an app shows what it opens in, when you last used it and its size, with **Update** for apps from Flathub.

### Settings

- **B** goes back to the list of categories first, then Home.
- **Select** leaves Settings and goes Home.

### The on-screen keyboard

When vaporTV needs text (a Wi-Fi password, a search), its keyboard slides up from the bottom. Each shortcut is shown on its key.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **A** | Type the highlighted key |
| **B** | Cancel |
| **X** | Delete |
| **Y** | Space |
| **L2** | Shift |
| **R2** | Symbols |
| **Start** | Enter |

These stay on the same buttons even if you've moved others in **Settings → Controller**. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard types into it too.

### Voice search

**R3** opens voice search: say a game or app ("Metroid Prime"), or "play Halo 2" to start it straight away. It's different in two places:

- On the **Music** tab, it searches your music instead.
- In the **Claude** app, it talks to Claude. **X** stops Claude speaking.

### The video player

Films and shows have their own controls: **A** plays and pauses, **L2 / R2** go 10 seconds back or on, **Y** is Audio & subtitles. See [The Video tab](video.md).

### Music

**L2** hides the Music screen's list of places, **Start** on a song opens its menu (Like, Start radio), and **R3** searches your music. See [Music](music.md).

## Moving buttons

You can put most actions on another button in **Settings → Controller**, under **Controls**.

1. Pick the action you want to move and press **A**. It says **Press a button…**.
2. Press the button you want for it.

That's it. If another action had that button, the two swap, and a note at the bottom says where the other one went. Each action always has a button, and no button has two.

What can be moved: **Select**, **Back**, **Favorite**, **Playlist**, **Game options**, **Previous tab**, **Next tab**, **Voice search**, **Next song**, **Left bar**, **Show the buttons** and **Settings**. They can go on A, B, X, Y, L1, R1, L2, R2, Select, Start, L3 or R3.

The two-button presses are at the end of **Controls**: **Close a game or app**, **Sound off or on** and **Report a problem**. Choose one, then hold the two buttons you want together. A pair that another of them already has is refused, with a note saying which. Close and Report still need holding for about a second; Sound off is a press.

What can't be moved: the **D-pad** and **sticks** always move, and the **Steam** button is Steam's.

**Reset to defaults** appears at the end of the list once you've moved something. It puts the two-button presses back too.

Your buttons are kept when vaporTV updates. If an update adds a new action, it goes on a free button and yours stay where you put them.

> **Tip:** to stop waiting for a button without changing anything, press the D-pad or wait a few seconds.

**Settings → Controller** also has **Sleep when switched off**: with a controller that uses a wireless USB receiver, switching it off puts the box to sleep and switching it on wakes it.

## On a keyboard

A USB or Bluetooth keyboard works too:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Arrows** | Move |
| **Enter** | Open what's highlighted |
| **Escape** or **Backspace** | Back |
| **Page Up / Page Down** | Previous or next tab |
| **F** | Favourite |
| **X** | Playlists |
| **G** | Game options |
| **L** | Show or hide a tab's list on the left |
| **H** | Show what each button does |
| **Menu** | Settings |

For TV remotes, see [TV remotes](remotes.md).

## If something's wrong

- **A button does the wrong thing:** check **Settings → Controller**. It may have been moved; **Reset to defaults** puts everything back.
- **vaporTV doesn't react after closing a game:** let go of every button. vaporTV waits until nothing is held, so the press that closed the game doesn't also act here.
- **The controller does nothing in a streaming website:** it needs a layout in Steam. See [Streaming apps](streaming-apps.md).
- **Hold Select + Start doesn't close a game:** hold both for a full second. If it still stays open, use **Settings → Help → Report a problem**.
- **The buttons on screen don't match your controller:** vaporTV goes by the maker the controller reports, and shows Xbox buttons for any it doesn't know. Report it with **Settings → Help → Report a problem**, saying which controller it is.
