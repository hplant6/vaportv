# Support vaporTV

vaporTV is free and open source, and it stays that way. If it's earned a place under your TV and you'd like to say thanks, you can buy me a coffee on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/vaportv). The QR code beside this page opens it on your phone.

> Tips are a thank-you, nothing more. They don't unlock anything, and every feature is for everyone.

## What's next

What I'm working towards. There are no dates: things land when they're ready, and plans can change.

### Themes

A proper theming engine, as flexible as ES-DE's but not built on XML. A theme will be able to change vaporTV's colours, fonts, backdrops and layout, so you can make it look the way you want and share your theme with other people.

## Ideas and problems

Ideas are welcome on [GitHub](https://github.com/hplant6/vaportv/issues). To report something that's broken, use **Settings → Help → Report a problem**.
